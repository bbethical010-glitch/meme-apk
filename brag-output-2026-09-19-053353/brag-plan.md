# Brag Plan: Meme Capsule

## What is this app?
Meme Capsule skips the algorithmic feed and delivers one random meme at a time from its D1/Reddit-powered meme network.

## The angle
An unhinged internet-native launch trailer for the anti-feed: no algorithm, no feed, no control. The joke is that Meme Capsule has reduced content discovery to one huge button and treats the resulting random meme like a high-stakes payload.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on an oppressive algorithmic feed: endless cards, “FOR YOU,” “STILL SCROLLING,” and “MORE OF THE SAME.” Hard-cut it away with **NO ALGORITHM. NO FEED.** and slam in **HIT ME**.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real Meme Capsule flow: the user taps **HIT ME**, then the hacker-style terminal logs run.
- The payload progress bar reaches 100% and a meme card slams into frame.
- The reaction picker detonates with **DANK!**, then the card is saved to the **VAULT** and the next random drop appears.

## Outro / punchline
The brand lockup lands with the exact tagline: **ONE TAP. ONE MEME. PURE CHAOS.** Include a Play Store badge and `memecapsule.wtf`.

## User flow worth showing
Open app → tap HIT ME → watch terminal loading → receive random meme → react DANK! → save to Vault.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: unhinged internet-native anti-feed product launch
- Interpretation: all-caps neo-brutalist type, hard cuts, flash/zoom transitions, dense rhythmic accents, and no shot longer than two seconds unless it is the final tagline hold.

## Format: horizontal (landscape) — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#131313`
- Accent: `#9b30ff`, `#f4c300`, `#dd0061`
- Text: `#e5e2e1`
- Display font: Anton with Oswald fallback
- Body font: Chivo with system fallback
- Strongest visual element: the yellow brutalist HIT ME button with pink offset shadow, terminal loading logs, and meme reaction chips

## Share copy (draft)
NO ALGORITHM. NO FEED. JUST HIT ME, GET A MEME, AND YELL DANK! Meme Capsule is one-tap chaos at memecapsule.wtf.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, punchy and upbeat
- Music treatment: start immediately at 0.28 volume, keep the bed driving the cuts, and let the final logo hit land around 22.92s before a short fade.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `composition/assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, ~109.96 BPM. Strong cues: 15.82s, 18.01s, 20.19s, 22.92s. Sequential accents can use beat-grid windows 4.10–6.28s for terminal lines and 10.93–13.64s for reaction/Vault beats.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; make the purple/yellow glow and icon presence breathe with low-frequency energy. No waveform or equalizer.
- SFX posture: dense but motion-matched; clicks for the tap, glitches for feed rejection and terminal, punch/impact for the meme slam and DANK reaction, bell for the final lockup.
- Audio-coupled moments: feed cards snap away on glitch; HIT ME tap lands on a click; terminal lines tick in sequence; meme card, DANK!, and Vault land on beats; final tagline hits on the strongest cue.
- Restraint rule: chaos comes from edit timing and copy, not constant noise; keep each SFX short and never bury the tagline.
