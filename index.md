# 1. About Meme Capsule

Meme Capsule is an entertainment app that lets users discover random meme images, save favorite memes locally, like memes, download memes, and share memes with others.

The app fetches meme content from online sources and presents it in a mobile-first interface. Meme Capsule is currently in the **internal testing phase** for Google Play Console.

Meme Capsule is designed to be lightweight and does not require users to create an account, log in, or submit personal profile information.

## 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

## 2.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app may store certain app activity information locally on your device to support app features.

This may include:

- Total memes fetched count

This information is stored locally using device/browser storage such as `localStorage` within the app environment.

Meme Capsule does not currently transmit this locally saved favorites or likes data to a Meme Capsule user account server.

# 4. Meme Content and Third-Party Sources

Meme Capsule fetches meme content from external online sources.

Current content sources may include:

- Meme Capsule backend endpoint
- Reddit-related meme API services
- Other external image URLs returned by meme APIs

Because meme content may originate from third-party sources, Meme Capsule does not fully control every item of content returned by those services.

The app attempts to avoid displaying content marked as NSFW or spoiler when such flags are provided by the source API. However, automated filtering may not be perfect.

Users should understand that external meme content may change over time and may include humor, satire, references, images, or text created by third parties.
