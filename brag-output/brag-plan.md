# Brag Plan: Meme Capsule

## What is this app?
Meme Capsule is a random meme-drop app: tap `HIT ME`, get a fresh payload, swipe through the chaos, react, save, share, and build a personal Vault.

## The angle
Treat the app like a wildly overfunded launch of an intentionally unserious infrastructure product. The joke is the contrast between hacker-grade language ("D1 shards", "R2 image payload", "optimal irreverence matrix") and the very simple promise: instant memes, zero algorithm.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen, terminal-style type: `THE INTERNET IS LOUD.` Then a yellow/pink impact stamp: `WE BUILT A CAPSULE.` The first two seconds should feel like a serious system announcement before the product lands.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The actual hero interaction: the oversized `HIT ME` button and the exact onboarding line `Pure memes. Zero algorithm.`
- The loading pipeline: `CONTACTING MEME NETWORK...` → progress fill → `SYNCING R2 IMAGE PAYLOAD...` → a meme result card.
- The real action rail: swipe, `LMAO`/`FIRE` reactions, `SECURE IN VAULT`, plus the Pro `TRIPLE DROP` tease.

## Outro / punchline
Three short commands stack: `SAVE IT. SHARE IT. HOARD IT.` The Meme Capsule lockup slams in with `THE DANK PAYLOAD IS READY.` The final frame should be a clean, shareable poster with the app name and icon.

## User flow worth showing
Open the app → tap `HIT ME` → watch the hacker-style loading pipeline → receive a meme drop → react/save/share or swipe to the next one.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: fake infrastructure launch for a meme delivery network
- Interpretation: Fast, loud, high-contrast and dead-serious about absurd backend terminology; hard cuts and impact hits, but readable holds for every claim.

## Duration: 20.2s

Use strong cues around 15.82s, 18.01s, and 20.19s for the payoff and logo. Sequential accents may align to 10.38s, 10.93s, 11.47s and 12.02s, but readable copy takes priority.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the purple capsule glow and yellow progress meter breathe with bass/RMS. No waveform or equalizer visuals.

- Restraint rule: narration must stay intelligible; no SFX on every text fragment and no bright repeated clicks.
