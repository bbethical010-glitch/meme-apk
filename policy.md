1. About This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy explains how Meme Capsule ("Meme Capsule", "we", "us", or "our") collects,
uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Meme Capsule Android application (the
"App").
Meme Capsule is a meme discovery and community application. The App allows users to view,
vote on, react to, save, download, and share memes, participate in community activity, and
report content.
The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address,
telephone number, or similar identity information to use its core features.
This Policy applies specifically to the Meme Capsule Android App. The separate Meme Capsule
website is governed by its own privacy policy.
The App is distributed worldwide. Privacy laws differ between jurisdictions, so additional rights
or requirements may apply depending on where you live and the circumstances of the
processing.
2. Information We Collect and Do Not Collect
2.1 Pseudonymous Device Identifier
Meme Capsule generates a pseudonymous identifier using technical and randomized data. The
identifier is used only where a stated purpose requires associating certain server-side activity
with the same App installation or device context over time.
Depending on the relevant processing, this may include:
• understanding content engagement and improving feed quality;
• supporting community activity and moderation;
• maintaining service security and reliability; and
• identifying patterns of activity relevant to preventing misuse or manipulation.
The identifier is not required for the basic operation of the App. Core activities such as viewing
memes and using features that operate locally on your device do not require you to provide an
account or directly identifying information.
Without the identifier, Meme Capsule may have less ability to associate separate server-side
activities with the same App installation or device context, which may affect certain analytics,
moderation, security, or operational functions.
The identifier is pseudonymous rather than anonymous because it may be used to associate
activity over time.
It is not intended to be your name, email address, telephone number, IMEI, MAC address,
Android hardware serial number, or Google Advertising ID.
Meme Capsule may also use the identifier, where appropriate, to help locate relevant server-
side records when processing a privacy or deletion request.
2.2 App Interaction and Analytics Data
Meme Capsule operates its own analytics and activity systems. Depending on how you use the
App, we may collect information about your interactions with content and App features, such as
viewing, skipping, reacting, voting, saving, downloading, sharing, and other engagement activity.
This information may be associated with a pseudonymous identifier, session information, App
version, content identifiers, timestamps, time spent viewing content, and related activity
information.
We use this information to understand content popularity and engagement, improve feed quality
and functionality, operate the App, and identify misuse or technical issues.
The information sent through our App activity systems does not intentionally include your name,
email address, telephone number, GPS location, or IP address as part of ordinary App analytics.
2.3 Reports and Moderation
If you report a meme or other content, we may process information such as:
• the relevant meme or content identifier;
• the reason for your report;
• any report text you submit;
• the time of the report; and
• the pseudonymous identifier associated with the submitting App installation or device
context.
Reports are reviewed through Meme Capsule's internal moderation processes.
Information relating to resolved reports may be retained as moderation, safety, security, or
operational information where appropriate.
2.4 Local App Data
Some features operate entirely on your device. These may include locally stored information
such as saved memes, Vaults, Mood Boards, local history, and local preferences.
This local information is not synchronised with the Meme Capsule backend.
You can generally remove local App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
This does not necessarily delete information that has already been processed on our servers.
2.5 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect at the App Level
Meme Capsule does not require or intentionally collect the following for its core App
functionality:
• your name;
• email address;
• telephone number;
• account credentials;
• precise location;
• contacts;
• camera or microphone data; or
• GPS or cell-tower location data.
Meme Capsule's application systems do not intentionally store client IP addresses as part of
ordinary App analytics records.
Our backend does not receive or store payment-card information, Google Play purchase tokens,
or Google Play transaction IDs for the PRO purchase.
This does not prevent third-party infrastructure or services from processing technical
information as described in this Policy.
