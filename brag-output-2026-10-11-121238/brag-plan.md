# Brag Plan: Meme Capsule

## What is this app?
Meme Capsule is an anti-algorithm arcade that dispenses one curated meme at a time, then lets you react, save, organise, and share it instantly.

## The angle
Treat Meme Capsule like an absurdly overproduced cyber-arcade launch: the internet has too much content, so the app gives you exactly one meme, with the drama of opening a rare item. The video should feel like a premium mobile game trailer crossed with a meme-page flex.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. A neon capsule icon locks on. Giant kinetic type: “NO FEED. NO DOOMSCROLL.” Then a hard gold/pink hit: “JUST HIT ME.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- Recreate the app’s waiting screen and the oversized HIT ME action.
- Show the hacker-console reveal: “HARVESTING D1 SHARDS… / FILTERING CRINGE FREQUENCIES…”
- Show a physical 3D meme card stack being dealt and swiped.
- Show the bottom action row: LIKE / VAULT / PIN / MORE, followed by a share-sheet style burst.
- Finish with a fast “ZERO ACCOUNTS / ZERO ALGORITHM / PURE CHAOS” lockup.

## Outro / punchline
“Open one. Send one. Leave before the algorithm notices.” End on MEME CAPSULE and “ONE TAP. ONE MEME. ZERO FLUFF.”

## User flow worth showing
Entry → tap HIT ME → hacker decryption → curated meme card appears → swipe/save/share.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: overproduced cyber-arcade launch trailer for a deliberately simple meme dispenser
- Interpretation: Fast hard cuts, chromatic aberration, tactile card motion, punchy typography, and restrained glitch accents. The UI must remain legible and feel like the real app rather than generic abstract motion.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 30 seconds (requested)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #131313
- Accent: #9b30ff, #f4c300, #dd0061
- Text: #e5e2e1 and white
- Display font: Anton
- Body font: Oswald / Chivo
- Strongest visual element: 4-card 3D perspective spring stack and the oversized HIT ME button

## Share copy (draft)
Meme Capsule: one tap, one hand-curated meme, zero algorithmic doomscrolling. Open one, send one, escape before the feed gets you.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer with polished arcade impacts
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: start immediately at a restrained level, build through the card reveal, duck slightly for the final line, and finish with a clean logo hit.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue JSON copied to `composition/assets/music/`; target strong cues around the 2.5s hook lock, 12s card reveal, and 26.5s logo close. Use beat-grid accents for the card and CTA sequence, but hold readable text.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the purple glow, card presence, and scanline intensity breathe with RMS/bass. No waveform or equalizer graphics.
- SFX posture: moderate and motion-matched: card slides, button clicks, one glitch accent, one final impact.
- Audio-coupled moments: type the hook with sparse clicks; button hit; card stack deal; CTA chips arriving; final logo impact.
- Restraint rule: avoid constant glitch noise and do not let SFX overpower the product copy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Anti-feed hook — 3s
Black/pitch canvas with grain, capsule icon, and a scanning magenta rule. “NO FEED.” then “NO DOOMSCROLL.” lands in Anton with gold/pink channel offsets. “JUST HIT ME.” slams in at the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three lines arrive in order and hold long enough to read.
Audio intent: immediate launch energy; sparse typed clicks followed by a hard button-like hit.
Audio-coupled idea: hook type appears on beat accents; major title hit around 2.5s.
Music: upbeat, dense but clean.
Transition mood: chaotic hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The dispenser — 4s
Recreate a phone-like app panel in the project palette. Top label: MEME CAPSULE. Center: oversized gold HIT ME button with pink offset shadow. Small badges: `STREAK 07`, `12 READY IN FIFO`. A cursor/tap indicator presses the button.
Sequential/interaction: yes — panel first, then button, then micro-badges.
Audio intent: tactile, arcade button press.
Audio-coupled idea: one precise click when HIT ME is pressed.
Transition mood: punch-in and chromatic wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Hacker decryption — 4s
Full-frame terminal-style loading overlay, with the actual product copy: “CONTACTING MEME NETWORK…”, “HARVESTING CLOUDFLARE D1 SHARDS…”, “FILTERING CRINGE FREQUENCIES…”. A counter scrambles from `03.14` to `69.69` while purple and pink scanlines sweep.
Sequential/interaction: yes — logs arrive one by one, counter ticks.
Audio intent: rising anticipation with a single digital glitch accent.
Audio-coupled idea: key/click accents for the first and final log; glitch at the counter resolve.
Transition mood: pixelate/glitch dissolve → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The drop — 6s
Three or four stylised meme cards appear as an actual 3D stack, with the front card carrying a bold placeholder meme composition: “WHEN THE BUILD PASSES FIRST TRY”. Rarity badge: `EPIC DROP`. The stack fans, the front card rocks, and one card swipes off-screen to reveal the next.
Sequential/interaction: yes — back cards deal in one by one, front card becomes dominant, then swipes.
Audio intent: satisfying card-deal rhythm with one heavy reveal impact.
Audio-coupled idea: card-slide/card-place accents; major reveal hit near 12s.
Transition mood: physical card shove → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Save, pin, share — 5s
The meme card compresses into the app’s actual CTA row: LIKE / VAULT / PIN / MORE. Each chip pops in with its brand colour. The MORE action opens a compact white menu with SHARE / DOWNLOAD / REPORT. A share-sheet ribbon flashes across the bottom with WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, and Instagram-style dots.
Sequential/interaction: yes — CTA chips arrive left to right, then MORE opens.
Audio intent: rhythmic, tactile, confident.
Audio-coupled idea: stagger non-text accents on consecutive beats; keep full CTA row held for readability.
Transition mood: snap zoom → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Anti-algorithm proof — 4s
Three statements occupy the frame with animated check marks and pulsing neon underlines: `ZERO ACCOUNTS`, `ZERO ALGORITHM`, `HAND-CURATED`. A tiny footer reads `D1 + R2 / SFW FILTERED / OFFLINE VAULT`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — statements reveal one by one, then lock together.
Audio intent: confidence and clarity after the chaotic middle.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle chimes on each check; no dense SFX.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Logo close — 4s
The supplied Meme Capsule icon rotates into centre with a gold ring and pink burst. Wordmark: MEME CAPSULE. Final copy: `ONE TAP. ONE MEME. ZERO FLUFF.` Under it: `OPEN ONE. SEND ONE. ESCAPE.`
Sequential/interaction: yes — icon, wordmark, tagline, then final line.
Audio intent: one cinematic logo impact with the music ducking under the final hold.
Audio-coupled idea: logo hit on the strongest closing cue around 26.5s.
Transition mood: final hold.

**Music mood for this video:** chaotic / upbeat arcade.
**Audio summary:** A clean upbeat bed drives tactile UI clicks, card motion, one glitch accent, and a decisive logo sting.
